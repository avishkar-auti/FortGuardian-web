Become a Volunteer

Join our mission to preserve Maharashtra's heritage and protect the environment

Why Volunteer with Fort Guardian?

Be part of a passionate community dedicated to preserving Maharashtra's rich heritage while promoting environmental sustainability. Your contribution makes a real difference in protecting our historical treasures for future generations.

Make a Difference

Contribute directly to heritage conservation and environmental protection initiatives.

Join Community

Connect with like-minded individuals who share your passion for history and nature.

Learn & Grow

Gain valuable skills in conservation, project management, and environmental awareness.

Recognition

Receive certificates and recognition for your valuable contributions to our mission.

Volunteer Opportunities

Cleaning Drive Leader

Lead and coordinate fort cleaning activities, ensuring proper waste management and site preservation.

Commitment: 1 day/month Experience: Beginner friendly

Eco-Camping Guide

Guide participants in sustainable camping practices and environmental education during overnight events.

Commitment: 2 days/month Experience: Intermediate

Heritage Educator

Share historical knowledge and stories about Maharashtra's forts during educational walks and events.

Commitment: 2 days/month Experience: History knowledge required

Documentation Volunteer

Capture events through photography and videography, help create promotional and educational content.

Commitment: Flexible Experience: Photography skills preferred

Safety Coordinator

Ensure participant safety during events, provide first aid assistance, and maintain safety protocols.

Commitment: 1 day/month Experience: First aid certification required

Social Media Ambassador

Promote our mission through social media, create engaging content, and help spread awareness.

Commitment: 5 hours/week Experience: Social media skills preferred
