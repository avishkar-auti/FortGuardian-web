About Fort Guardian

Preserving Maharashtra's heritage for future generations

Our Mission

Fort Guardian is dedicated to preserving and protecting Maharashtra's rich historical heritage through community-driven conservation efforts. We believe that every fort, every stone, and every story is a testament to our glorious past that deserves to be safeguarded for future generations.

Our mission extends beyond mere preservation - we aim to create awareness, foster environmental responsibility, and build a community of passionate individuals who share our vision of sustainable heritage tourism.

Our Story

The Beginning

Fort Guardian was born from the vision of a small group of heritage enthusiasts who were deeply concerned about the deteriorating condition of Maharashtra's historic forts. In 2020, we started with just 5 volunteers and a simple mission: to make a difference.

Growing Community

What started as a small initiative quickly grew into a passionate community of over 500 volunteers. Our dedication to combining heritage conservation with environmental responsibility attracted like-minded individuals from all walks of life.

Making Impact

Today, we have successfully cleaned and preserved over 25 forts, organized 50+ events, and collected more than 10,000 kg of waste. Our impact extends beyond numbers - we've created lasting change in how people perceive heritage conservation.

Our Values

Environmental Stewardship

We believe in preserving not just historical structures but also the natural environment that surrounds them. Every action we take is guided by sustainable practices and eco-friendly principles.

Community Collaboration

Heritage conservation is a collective responsibility. We work closely with local communities, government agencies, and fellow organizations to create lasting positive change.

Education & Awareness

We believe that knowledge is the foundation of conservation. Through our events and programs, we educate participants about history, archaeology, and environmental conservation.

Integrity & Transparency

Our operations are guided by the highest standards of integrity and transparency. We maintain open communication with all stakeholders and ensure accountability in all our activities.

Our Journey

2020

Foundation

Fort Guardian was established with a mission to preserve Maharashtra's historical heritage. Our first event was a small cleaning drive at Raigad Fort with 15 volunteers.

2021

First Major Campaign

Launched our first major heritage conservation campaign, focusing on Sinhagad Fort. The event attracted 100+ volunteers and marked our entry into organized heritage tourism.

2022

Eco-Tourism Initiative

Introduced sustainable camping programs to promote responsible tourism. Our eco-camping events became popular among nature enthusiasts and history lovers alike.

2023

Recognition & Awards

Received the "Outstanding Heritage Conservation" award from the Maharashtra Tourism Board. Our work was recognized by various government and non-government organizations.

2024

Expansion & Growth

Expanded our operations to cover 25+ forts across Maharashtra. Established partnerships with local communities and environmental organizations.

2025

Future Vision

Continuing our mission with renewed energy and expanded programs. Planning to reach 50 forts and 1000+ volunteers by the end of 2025.
