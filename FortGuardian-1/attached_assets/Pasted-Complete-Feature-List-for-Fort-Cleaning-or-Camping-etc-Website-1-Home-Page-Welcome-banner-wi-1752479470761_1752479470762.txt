Complete Feature List for "Fort Cleaning or Camping etc" Website
🏠 1. Home Page
Welcome banner with scenic images

Brief intro about your mission (cleaning forts, promoting eco-camping)

Call-to-action buttons (e.g., “View Upcoming Events”, “Join Campaign”)

📅 2. Events & Campaigns Page
List of upcoming events (camping, fort cleaning, trekking)

Each event includes:

Event Name & Type (e.g., Camping, Cleaning Drive)

Location with map

Date & Time

Registration Deadline

Number of seats left (optional)

“Register Now” button

Filter or search by event type/date (can be added later with JavaScript)

📝 3. Registration Form
Form fields: Full Name, Email, Phone Number, Event selection, Emergency Contact

Submission confirmation page or message

Optional: download event guidelines after registration

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 4. Volunteer/Member Section
Info on how to become a regular volunteer

Benefits of joining your community

“Join as Volunteer” form

🖼️ 5. Photo/Video Gallery
Photos from past campaigns/camping experiences

Optionally group by event

Embedded YouTube videos (if any)

💬 6. Testimonials
Reviews from past participants or volunteers

Star ratings or quote layout

📌 7. About Us
Your mission, story, and team

Timeline of achievements or major past events

📞 8. Contact Page
Contact form: Name, Email, Message

Your social links (Instagram, Facebook, etc.)

Embedded Google Map (for meetup points or HQ)
