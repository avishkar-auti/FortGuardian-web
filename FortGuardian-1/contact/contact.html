Frequently Asked Questions

How can I participate in events?

You can register for events through our events page. Most events are open to all age groups with basic fitness requirements.

Are events free to participate?

Yes, all our heritage conservation events are completely free. We believe in making heritage accessible to everyone.

What should I bring to events?

We provide a detailed packing list after registration. Generally, you'll need comfortable clothes, water, and personal items.

How can I become a volunteer?

Visit our volunteer page to fill out the application form. We welcome volunteers of all backgrounds and experience levels.
